This is a synthetic placeholder used only to exercise the retrieval and

A second theme in this placeholder episode is the difference between a
"north star metric" and an "activation metric" -- the north star describes
long-term value delivered, while activation describes whether a new user
reached a first moment of that value. Conflating the two leads teams to
optimize onboarding for the wrong signal.
